### United Airlines

## SitecoreAI campaign scope correction

The earlier audit established factual accuracy, not that each story proves the current SitecoreAI offering. A full Sage run selected FIS and produced XP-led features/FAQ/CTA after an existing-XP relationship answer; the marketer rejected that campaign focus.

They remain unchanged.

The current [official SitecoreAI platform overview](https://www.sitecore.com/platform), fetched 2026-11-05, says agents draft/tag/translate/activate content within workflows, with people approving/publishing; it names connected content/data/personalization/optimization and CMS visual authoring, plus role-based permissions, approval gates and audit logs. It is current offering evidence; Brand Kit is voice guidance, not proof of a capability.

The local index adds an editorial proof-fit classification, separate from source readiness: XP-led Microsoft/Fujitsu/FIS and classic XM-led WellSpan are explicit-legacy-only; United/Pet Supplies Plus are relevant SaaS foundation cases, not proof of present AI-agent outcomes. No pilot source currently establishes sitecoreai-direct results. Historical metrics/products/quotes are preserved in the six story documents, not rewritten as SitecoreAI evidence.
